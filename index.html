Need to know

Frequently asked questions.

Are the StudyKit tools free?

Yes. All seven basic tools are free to use without an account.

Do I need to register?

No. You can use the available tools directly in your browser.

Is my text uploaded?

The text tools process your text locally in your browser. Your text is not intentionally sent to our servers by these tools.

What is StudyKit Pro?

StudyKit Pro is a planned optional membership with additional features and an ad-free experience. It is not available for purchase yet.

Are advertisements active?

No. Advertising spaces are being prepared. Ads will only be added after the relevant advertising account is approved.

Can I use StudyKit on my phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.

Your information

Privacy Policy

StudyKit's basic text and calculation tools run in your browser. Text entered into these tools is not intentionally uploaded to StudyKit servers.

If advertising, analytics, accounts or payment services are added in the future, those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. This policy must be reviewed and updated before those services launch.

Cookies and third parties

Third-party services, when introduced, may use cookies or similar technologies. We will disclose relevant services and choices before enabling them.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact details provided by the StudyKit website owner once published.
