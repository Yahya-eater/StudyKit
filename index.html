Frequently asked questions

Are these study tools free?

Yes. The tools on this page are free to use and do not require registration.

Is my text uploaded or saved?

No. The word counter and text converter process text locally in your browser. Avoid entering private or sensitive information on any public website.

Can I use StudyKit on my phone?

Yes. The page is designed to adapt to phones, tablets and desktop screens.

How accurate is the reading-time estimate?

It is an estimate based on your selected reading speed. Actual reading time depends on the difficulty of the text and your pace.

Can StudyKit replace checking my school's grading rules?

No. The grade calculator gives a simple percentage. Your school may use its own grading scale or weighting system.
