Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to StudyKit. We respect your privacy and aim to protect the information of everyone who visits our website.

2. Information We Collect

StudyKit's basic educational calculators and text tools are designed to work directly in your browser. We do not intentionally collect or store the text entered into these tools on our own servers.

Our hosting provider and third-party services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and usage data.

3. Cookies and Advertising

StudyKit may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, in the future. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements and measure their performance.

Advertising services are not currently confirmed as active. This policy will be updated when advertising is introduced.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

StudyKit may introduce optional paid subscriptions. Payments, if introduced, will be processed by third-party payment providers. StudyKit will not intentionally store full payment card details on its own servers.

Paid subscriptions are not currently active.

5. Children's Privacy

StudyKit is intended for students and general educational use. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online services.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and data practices. Please review their policies before using them.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the website. However, no internet transmission or online service can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as StudyKit develops. Changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the StudyKit website administrator through the contact details published on our website.
